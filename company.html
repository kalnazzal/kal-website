About KAL Construction and Services

A New York-based construction management and general contracting company built on hands-on oversight — now also serving clients in San Diego, CA.

KAL Construction and Services, Inc. is a New York-based construction company specializing in residential and commercial renovations, construction management, and general contracting services — now also serving clients in San Diego, CA.

Established in 2004, the company is led by an experienced Construction Manager with a strong track record of delivering projects through effective planning, coordination, quality control, and project management.

Work spans NYC, Rockland County, Westchester County, and San Diego, CA, covering everything from interior renovations to full construction-management oversight of small- and mid-sized capital improvement projects.
